# References and sources by area

Read only the section for your area code: the first letters of the ficha codes.

- **House references** are in `C:/Users/Administrator/Desktop/CLAUDE SKILLS/juridico-pt/skills/juridico-pt/references/`.
- **The amounts of the year** are in `valores-2026.md`, in that same folder.
- **Sources:** read each diploma's text as currently in force, from the PGDL, diariodarepublica.pt or EUR-Lex.

## ADM — administrative law
- **House:** `contratacao-publica.md`, `multas.md`, `licenciamento-setorial.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** CPA, CPTA, CCP.
- **Notes:**
  - The 2015 CPTA reform abolished the "ação administrativa especial" and the "recurso contencioso". The current form is the ação administrativa.
  - The CPA is DL 4/2015.

## AMB — environment and energy
- **House:** `licenciamento-setorial.md`, `compliance.md`, `uniao-europeia.md`.
- **Diplomas:**
  - Lei 19/2014 (Lei de Bases do Ambiente);
  - DL 151-B/2013 (RJAIA);
  - DL 147/2008 (environmental liability);
  - Lei 50/2006 (environmental offences);
  - DL 15/2022 (electricity system);
  - the EU acts on EUR-Lex.

## BAN — banking and financial markets
- **House:** `bancario.md`, `garantias.md`, `cobrancas.md`.
- **Diplomas:**
  - the RGICSF, now DL 298/92 as amended;
  - the Código dos Valores Mobiliários;
  - DL 133/2009 (consumer credit);
  - DL 74-A/2017 (mortgage credit);
  - Banco de Portugal notices.

## CIV — civil law and contracts
- **House:** `contratos.md`, `cobrancas.md`, `garantias.md`, `contencioso.md`, `imobiliario.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** CC, CPC.
- **Diplomas:** DL 446/85 (standard terms), DL 269/98 (injunction), DL 62/2013 (late payment).

## CNS — consumer law
- **House:** `consumo.md`.
- **Diplomas:**
  - DL 84/2021 (sales and digital content);
  - DL 24/2014 (distance contracts);
  - Lei 24/96 (Lei de Defesa do Consumidor);
  - Lei 23/96 (essential public services);
  - DL 156/2005 (complaints book);
  - Lei 144/2015 (ADR).
- **Note:** the EU ODR platform was closed on 20/07/2025 (Reg. 2024/3228).

## COM — commercial and company law
- **House:** `societario.md`, `contratos.md`, `cobrancas.md`, `garantias.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** CSC, CIRE.
- **Diplomas:** DL 178/86 (agency), DL 231/81 (associação em participação).

## CON — competition
- **House:** `concorrencia.md`, `uniao-europeia.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** TFUE.
- **Diplomas:**
  - Lei 19/2012, as amended by Lei 17/2022;
  - Reg. 1/2003;
  - the EU block exemptions in force in 2026 (check on EUR-Lex: some expired in 2022–2026 and were replaced).

## DES — sports law
- **House:** `laboral.md`, `contratos.md`.
- **Diplomas:**
  - Lei 54/2017 (sports employment contract);
  - Lei 5/2007 (Lei de Bases da Atividade Física e do Desporto): check whether it has been replaced;
  - Lei 74/2013 (TAD);
  - the FIFA regulations from fifa.com (RSTP, FFAR).

## DIG — data protection and digital
- **House:** `rgpd.md`, `digital-ue.md`, `penal-cibercrime.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** RGPD.
- **Diplomas:**
  - Lei 58/2019;
  - Lei 41/2004 (e-privacy);
  - the AI Act (Reg. 2024/1689);
  - the DSA (Reg. 2022/2065);
  - the NIS2 transposition in Portugal: check which diploma it is.

## EST — foreigners and nationality
- **House:** `estrangeiros.md`.
- **Diplomas:**
  - Lei 23/2007, as amended by DL 37-A/2024; Lei 61/2025 and Lei 62/2026 are reported, so confirm them;
  - Lei 37/2006 (EU citizens);
  - Lei 37/81 (nationality): check whether the 2025/2026 reform is in force;
  - Lei 27/2008 (asylum): check the effect of the EU Migration Pact from 12/06/2026.
- **Note:** the AIMA replaced the SEF.

## FAM — family and children
- **House:** `familia.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** CC.
- **Diplomas:** Lei 141/2015 (RGPTC), Lei 147/99 (LPCJP), Lei 112/2009 (domestic violence), the legal regime of adoption (Lei 143/2015).

## FIS — tax law
- **House:** `fiscal.md`, `fiscal-pessoal.md`, `contencioso-tributario.md`, `iva-internacional.md`, `faturacao.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** LGT, CPPT, RGIT, CIRS, CIRC, CIMI.
- **Sources:**
  - the codes on the Portal das Finanças (CIVA, CIS, CIMT, EBF, RJAT);
  - the 2026 State Budget for rates.

## IMO — real estate and leases
- **House:** `arrendamento.md`, `imobiliario.md`, `herancas.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** CC, RJUE, CIMI.
- **Sources:** the NRAU is PGDL nid 691.
- **Notes:**
  - Since Lei 56/2023, the BNA is the BAS (Balcão do Arrendatário e do Senhorio, art. 15.º-A NRAU).
  - DL 10/2024 changed urban licensing and deeds.

## INS — insolvency and recovery
- **House:** `insolvencia.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** CIRE (PGDL nid 85), as amended by Lei 9/2022.
- **Diplomas:** the RERE (Lei 8/2018).

## PEN — criminal law and offences
- **House:** `penal-cibercrime.md`, `multas.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** CP, CPP, RGCO.
- **Diplomas:** Lei 109/2009 (cybercrime), Lei 37/2015 (criminal record).

## PI — intellectual property
- **House:** `pi.md`, `compliance.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** CPI (DL 110/2018), CDADC.
- **Diplomas:**
  - DL 252/94 (software);
  - Reg. 2017/1001 (EU trade mark);
  - Reg. 2022/720 (vertical block exemption).
- **Note:** Lei 93/2021 is the whistleblower law, not trade secrets. Trade secrets are in the CPI.

## PRC — civil procedure
- **House:** `contencioso.md`, `cobrancas.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** CPC, CC.
- **Diplomas:**
  - the RCP (custas);
  - Lei 62/2013 (LOSJ);
  - Reg. 1215/2012 and Reg. 2020/1784 (service), from EUR-Lex.

## SAU — health law and medical liability
- **House:** `seguros.md`, `compliance.md`.
- **Diplomas:**
  - Lei 15/2014 (patients' rights);
  - Lei 95/2019 (Lei de Bases da Saúde);
  - Lei 67/2007 (State non-contractual liability);
  - DL 72/2008 (insurance contract);
  - Lei 21/2014 (clinical research);
  - DL 279/2009 / DL 127/2014 (licensing of health providers);
  - Lei 98/2009 (work accidents).

## SUC — succession
- **House:** `herancas.md`, `sucessorio-internacional.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** CC, CPC.
- **Note:** the inventory proceedings are in the CPC (arts. 1082.º ff., Lei 117/2019), with the court/notary split in art. 1083.º.
- **Diplomas:** Reg. 650/2012, from EUR-Lex; the Código do Imposto do Selo, for gifts and inheritances.

## TRA — labour law
- **House:** `laboral.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** CT (PGDL nid 1047), as amended by Lei 13/2023 (Agenda do Trabalho Digno).
- **Diplomas:** the CPT, Lei 98/2009 (work accidents), Lei 102/2009 (health and safety).
- **Note:** a 2026 labour reform was reported as pending, so do not assume it.

## UE — European Union law
- **House:** `uniao-europeia.md`, `digital-ue.md`, `fundos-europeus.md`.
- **Epígrafes:** TFUE.
- **Sources:** EUR-Lex and curia.europa.eu (Rules of Procedure of the Court of Justice and of the General Court).

## URB — urban planning and land use

- **Epígrafes:** RJUE.
- **Diplomas:**
  - DL 10/2024 (urban-planning Simplex) and later changes;
  - DL 80/2015 (RJIGT);
