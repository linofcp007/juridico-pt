# Requerimento de Junta Médica e Impugnação de Atestado de Incapacidade

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito da Saúde e Responsabilidade Médica · **Código:** SAU-021
> **EN:** Request for a Medical Board and Challenge to a Disability Certificate. Drafts the request to convene a medical board (junta médica) and/or the challenge to a disability certificate (atestado de incapacidade) issued by a doctor or by the National Institute for Rehabilitation (INR), on the grounds of disagreement with the degree of disability assigned or the methodology used.
