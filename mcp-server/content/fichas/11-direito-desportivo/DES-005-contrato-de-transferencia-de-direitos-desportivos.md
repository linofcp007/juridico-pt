# Contrato de Transferência de Direitos Desportivos

Drafts a permanent transfer agreement for an athlete between clubs, structuring payment of the transfer fee, sell-on fee clauses, solidarity percentages, deferred payment obligations, conditions precedent and subsequent, and compliance with FIFA and national federation regulations.

**CLÁUSULA 5.ª — SELL-ON FEE**
