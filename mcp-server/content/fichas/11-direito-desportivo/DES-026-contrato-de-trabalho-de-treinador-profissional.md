# Contrato de Trabalho de Treinador Profissional

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito Desportivo · **Código:** DES-026
> **EN:** Employment Contract for a Professional Coach. Drafts a sports employment contract for a professional coach under Articles 35 et seq. of Law No. 54/2017 of 14 July (LCTD) and the FPF/LPFP Coaches Regulations, including base remuneration, performance bonuses, a release clause, assignment of image rights and regulatory obligations (coaching card, insurance).

### ETAPA 3 — ESTRUTURA DO CONTRATO
