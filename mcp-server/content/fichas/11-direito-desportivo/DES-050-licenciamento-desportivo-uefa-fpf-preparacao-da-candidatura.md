# Licenciamento Desportivo UEFA/FPF — Preparação da Candidatura

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito Desportivo · **Código:** DES-050
> **EN:** UEFA/FPF Club Licensing — Preparing the Application. Prepares the preparation plan and supporting documentation for the application for a UEFA club licence (UEFA Club Licensing and Financial Fair Play Regulations) and an FPF/LPFP licence, covering the sporting, infrastructure, personnel and administrative, financial and legal criteria, and identifying the mandatory documents and submission deadlines.
