# Impugnação Judicial de Liquidação Adicional de IRC

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito Fiscal e Tributário · **Código:** FIS-035
> **EN:** Judicial Challenge to an Additional IRC Assessment. Prepares a statement of claim for a judicial challenge (impugnação judicial) to an additional IRC assessment, based on the unlawfulness of the technical correction or of the tax inspection that gave rise to it, under Articles 99 et seq. of the CPPT and Article 100 of the LGT.
