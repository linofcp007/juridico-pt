# Arbitragem Tributária no CAAD — Pedido de Constituição de Tribunal Arbitral Complexo

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito Fiscal e Tributário · **Código:** FIS-036
> **EN:** Tax Arbitration at the CAAD — Request to Set Up an Arbitral Tribunal in a Complex Case. Prepares the request to set up a collective arbitral tribunal at the Administrative Arbitration Centre (CAAD), for disputes worth more than €10,000, with full legal reasoning under Decree-Law No. 10/2011 of 20 January (RJAT) and the Ministerial Orders binding the AT to arbitration (Portarias de vinculação).
