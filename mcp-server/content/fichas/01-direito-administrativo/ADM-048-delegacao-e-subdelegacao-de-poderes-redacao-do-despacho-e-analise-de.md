# Delegação e Subdelegação de Poderes — Redação do Despacho e Análise de Validade (art. 44.º CPA)

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito Administrativo · **Código:** ADM-048
> **EN:** Delegation and Sub-Delegation of Powers — Drafting the Order and Assessing Validity (Article 44 CPA). Drafts an order (despacho) delegating and sub-delegating administrative powers under Articles 44 to 50 of the CPA (Decree-Law No. 4/2015) and assesses the validity of acts performed under a delegation, including the limits, the publication requirement and the consequences of breaching the delegation rules.
