# Comunicação de Crime ao Ministério Público — Participação de Notícia de Crime

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito Penal e Contraordenacional · **Código:** PEN-051
> **EN:** Reporting an Offence to the Public Prosecutor's Office — Crime Report. Drafts the crime report (participação de notícia de crime) addressed to the Public Prosecutor's Office (DIAP — Department of Investigation and Prosecution) or the police authorities under Articles 241 et seq. of the Portuguese Code of Criminal Procedure, including a statement of the facts, the legal classification and the available evidence, for public and semi-public offences.
