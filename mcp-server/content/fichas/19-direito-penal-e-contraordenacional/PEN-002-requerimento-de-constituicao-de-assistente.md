# Requerimento de Constituição de Assistente

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito Penal e Contraordenacional · **Código:** PEN-002
> **EN:** Application to Be Admitted as Assistente. Generates a standalone application to be admitted as assistente (auxiliary prosecutor) in pending criminal proceedings, based on Article 68 of the CPP (Code of Criminal Procedure), identifying the applicant's standing, the appropriate procedural stage and the powers that admission confers.
