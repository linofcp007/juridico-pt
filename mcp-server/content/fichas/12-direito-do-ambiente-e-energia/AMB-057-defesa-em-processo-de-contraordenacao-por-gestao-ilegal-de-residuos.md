# Defesa em Processo de Contraordenação por Gestão Ilegal de Resíduos (DL 102-D/2020)

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito do Ambiente e Energia · **Código:** AMB-057
> **EN:** Defence in Administrative Offence Proceedings for Illegal Waste Management (DL 102-D/2020). Drafts the written defence in administrative offence proceedings for the deposit, storage, transport or treatment of waste without a valid permit, under Decree-Law No. 102-D/2020 of 10 December (RGGR) and Law No. 50/2006 of 29 August (LQCA), including analysis of the waste classification, a challenge to the allegation of illegal management and a regularisation proposal.
