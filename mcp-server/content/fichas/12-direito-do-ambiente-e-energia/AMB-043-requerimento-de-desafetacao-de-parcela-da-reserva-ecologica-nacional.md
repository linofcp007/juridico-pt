# Requerimento de Desafetação de Parcela da Reserva Ecológica Nacional (REN)

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito do Ambiente e Energia · **Código:** AMB-043
> **EN:** Application for the Release of a Parcel from the National Ecological Reserve (REN). Drafts the application for authorisation of a use or action in a National Ecological Reserve (REN) area that does not constitute formal release (desafetação), or the request for a delimitation/exclusion procedure, under Decree-Law No. 166/2008 of 22 August (legal regime of the REN) and the powers of the CCDRs, including the descriptive report, the justification of compatibility with the REN's ecological functions and the framework under the Municipal Master Plan (PDM).
