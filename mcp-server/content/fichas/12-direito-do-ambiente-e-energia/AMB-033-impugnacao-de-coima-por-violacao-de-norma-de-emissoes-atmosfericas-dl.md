# Impugnação de Coima por Violação de Norma de Emissões Atmosféricas (DL 39/2018)

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito do Ambiente e Energia · **Código:** AMB-033
> **EN:** Challenge to a Fine for Breach of Air Emission Rules (DL 39/2018). Drafts the written defence and, in the alternative, the judicial appeal against the fine imposed for breach of Decree-Law No. 39/2018 of 11 June (regime for integrated prevention and control of air pollution — PCIP), including analysis of the emission limit values (VLE), the validity of the sampling and a challenge to the attribution of fault.
