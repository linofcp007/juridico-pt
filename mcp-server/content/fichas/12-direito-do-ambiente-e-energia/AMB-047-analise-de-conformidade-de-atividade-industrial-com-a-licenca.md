> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito do Ambiente e Energia · **Código:** AMB-047
> **EN:** Compliance Analysis of an Industrial Activity with its Environmental Licence (PCIP/RJAIA). Drafts a legal and technical-legal analysis of an industrial installation's compliance with the conditions of its environmental licence, under Decree-Law No. 127/2013 of 30 August (PRIP — Integrated Pollution Prevention and Control), Decree-Law No. 39/2018 and the Single Environmental Permit (TUA, Decree-Law 75/2015), identifying non-conformities, risks of licence suspension and a regularisation plan.
