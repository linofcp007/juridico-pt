# Reclamação Graciosa e Recurso Hierárquico de Ato Administrativo Desfavorável (arts. 191.º a 199.º CPA)

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito Administrativo · **Código:** ADM-054
> **EN:** Administrative Complaint and Hierarchical Appeal against an Unfavourable Administrative Act (Articles 191 to 199 CPA). Drafts an administrative complaint (reclamação graciosa) and a hierarchical appeal (recurso hierárquico) against an unfavourable administrative act, under Articles 191 to 199 of the CPA (Decree-Law No. 4/2015 of 7 January), including an analysis of the time limit, the defects of the act and the administrative challenge strategy before turning to the courts.
