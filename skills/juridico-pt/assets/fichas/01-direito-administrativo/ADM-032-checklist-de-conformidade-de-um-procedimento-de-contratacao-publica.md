# Checklist de Conformidade de um Procedimento de Contratação Pública

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito Administrativo · **Código:** ADM-032
> **EN:** Compliance Checklist for a Public Procurement Procedure. Generates a structured legal compliance checklist for a public procurement procedure, checking every CCP requirement from the decision to contract through to the conclusion of the contract and identifying points of risk or non-compliance, under the CCP and the European Directives.
