# Pedido de Certificação de Título Executivo Europeu

> **Âmbito:** UE · **Área:** Direito da União Europeia · **Código:** UE-024
> **EN:** Application for Certification as a European Enforcement Order. Prepares the application to certify a Portuguese court decision as a European Enforcement Order (TEE) under Regulation (EC) No 805/2004, allowing its direct enforcement in another Member State without the need for exequatur. It covers uncontested claims in civil and commercial matters.
