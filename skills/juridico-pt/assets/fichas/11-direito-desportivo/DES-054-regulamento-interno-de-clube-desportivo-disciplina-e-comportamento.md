# Regulamento Interno de Clube Desportivo — Disciplina e Comportamento

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito Desportivo · **Código:** DES-054
> **EN:** Internal Regulations of a Sports Club — Discipline and Conduct. Prepares internal disciplinary and conduct regulations for a professional sports club, in compliance with Article 99 of the Labour Code, Article 17 of Law No. 54/2017 (LCTD) and the FPF/LPFP disciplinary regulations, defining the offences, the applicable sanctions, the internal disciplinary procedure and the defence guarantees of athletes and other sports staff.
