# Parecer sobre Justa Causa de Rescisão em Contrato Desportivo

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito Desportivo · **Código:** DES-013
> **EN:** Opinion on Just Cause for Termination of a Sports Contract. Issues a reasoned legal opinion on whether certain facts constitute just cause for termination of a sports employment contract by the athlete or the club, assessing the risk of an order to pay compensation, the prospects before the TAD and the most appropriate strategy (termination, negotiation or seeing the contract through to its end).
