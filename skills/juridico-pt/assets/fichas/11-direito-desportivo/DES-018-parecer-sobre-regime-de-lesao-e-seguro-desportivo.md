# Parecer sobre Regime de Lesão e Seguro Desportivo

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito Desportivo · **Código:** DES-018
> **EN:** Opinion on Injury Rules and Sports Insurance. Issues an opinion on the athlete's rights in the event of an injury sustained during sporting activity, analysing whether the contract and remuneration continue during incapacity, the club's sports insurance obligations, the applicable Social Security (Segurança Social) regime and the situations in which the athlete may terminate for just cause owing to failures in health and safety obligations.
