# Parecer sobre Servidões e Restrições Ambientais de um Terreno

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito do Ambiente e Energia · **Código:** AMB-021
> **EN:** Legal Opinion on Environmental Easements and Restrictions on Land. Drafts a legal opinion on the administrative easements (servidões administrativas) and public-interest restrictions of an environmental nature burdening a plot of land — RAN (National Agricultural Reserve), REN (National Ecological Reserve), the Natura 2000 network, protection zones for beds and banks of watercourses, protected landscapes — analysing the impact on the possibilities for use, building on and transacting the property.
