# Parecer sobre Autoconsumo Coletivo e Comunidades de Energia Renovável

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito do Ambiente e Energia · **Código:** AMB-032
> **EN:** Legal Opinion on Collective Self-Consumption and Renewable Energy Communities. Drafts a legal opinion on the formation and operation of a renewable energy community (CER) or a collective self-consumption unit, under Decree-Law No. 15/2022 of 14 January (RJAERE), Decree-Law No. 162/2019 of 25 October and ERSE regulations, including energy-sharing requirements, the CER governance model and obligations towards the DGEG and the distribution system operator.
