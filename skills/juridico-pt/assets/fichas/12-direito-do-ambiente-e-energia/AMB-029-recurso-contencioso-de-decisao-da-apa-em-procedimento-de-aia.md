# Recurso Contencioso de Decisão da APA em Procedimento de AIA

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito do Ambiente e Energia · **Código:** AMB-029
> **EN:** Judicial Challenge to an APA Decision in an AIA Procedure. Drafts the statement of claim in a special administrative action challenging an unfavourable Environmental Impact Statement (DIA) or the APA's decision in an environmental impact assessment (AIA) procedure, under the Legal Regime for Environmental Impact Assessment (RJAIA, Decree-Law No. 151-B/2013 of 31 October, as currently worded) and the Code of Procedure in the Administrative Courts (CPTA).
