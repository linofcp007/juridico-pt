# Requerimento de Licença de Produção de Energia Eólica Offshore

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito do Ambiente e Energia · **Código:** AMB-031
> **EN:** Application for an Offshore Wind Energy Production Licence. Drafts the application for the grant of a production permit for an offshore wind energy project, under Decree-Law No. 15/2022 of 14 January (RJAERE), Decree-Law No. 38/2015 (framework for maritime spatial planning and management policy) and the National Maritime Space Regulation, identifying the components of the marine AIA, the licence for private use of maritime space (TUPEM) and coordination with the Directorate-General for Maritime Policy (DGPM) and the APA.
