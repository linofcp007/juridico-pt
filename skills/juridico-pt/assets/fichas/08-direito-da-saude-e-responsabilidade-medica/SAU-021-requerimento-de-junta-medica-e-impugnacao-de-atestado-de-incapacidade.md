# Requerimento de Junta Médica e Impugnação de Atestado de Incapacidade

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito da Saúde e Responsabilidade Médica · **Código:** SAU-021
> **EN:** Request for a Medical Board and Challenge to a Disability Certificate. Drafts the request for assessment or reassessment by a medical board and/or the challenge to the degree of disability assigned, in particular the hierarchical appeal against the multi-purpose medical disability certificate (atestado médico de incapacidade multiúso) issued by the disability assessment medical board (JMAI) under Decree-Law No. 202/96, on the grounds of disagreement with the degree of disability assigned or the methodology used.
