# Parecer sobre Contagem de Prazos de Residência Legal — Naturalização e AR Permanente

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito dos Estrangeiros e Nacionalidade · **Código:** EST-045
> **EN:** Legal Opinion on Counting Periods of Legal Residence — Naturalisation and Permanent Residence. Drafts a detailed legal opinion on how periods of legal residence are counted for the purposes of naturalisation (Article 6 of Law 37/81) and a permanent residence permit (Article 80 of Law 23/2007), including analysis of gaps, interruptions, periods of temporary protection, periods with precarious documentation and the impact of prolonged absences from the territory.
