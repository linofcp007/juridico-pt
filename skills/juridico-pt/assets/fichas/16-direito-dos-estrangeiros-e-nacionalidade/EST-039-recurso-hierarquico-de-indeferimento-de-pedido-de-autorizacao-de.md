# Recurso Hierárquico de Indeferimento de Pedido de Autorização de Residência (AIMA)

> **Âmbito:** nacional · **Área:** Direito dos Estrangeiros e Nacionalidade · **Código:** EST-039
> **EN:** Administrative Appeal against Refusal of a Residence Permit Application (AIMA). Prepares the administrative appeal (recurso hierárquico) against AIMA's decision refusing a residence permit application, under Articles 193 et seq. of the Code of Administrative Procedure (CPA), in conjunction with Articles 149 et seq. of Law No. 23/2007 on the means of challenging decisions concerning foreign nationals.
